Why Enkuan Stay

Built so every guest is welcomed fairly

Past climate summits were marked by price gouging and cancelled bookings. Enkuan Stay is designed from the start to prevent both.

Inspected and certified

Every home is checked on site for safety, water, backup power and internet.

Published rates

Rates are registered in advance, by class. No surge pricing during the conference.

Booking guarantee

A confirmed stay is held at its price. If a host cancels, we move you to an equal or better home.

Invoices that clear

Formal invoices for governments, UN bodies and NGOs, with card or bank transfer payment.
